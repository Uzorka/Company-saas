Rules that apply to all of it
Nothing decorativeIf an animation doesn't explain origin, confirm an action or show a change, it isn't in the product.
420ms ceilingOnly check-in success and location matching reach it. Everything else is 320ms or less.
Reduced motion keeps feedbackTransforms drop, opacity and colour stay. A user who disables motion still sees that their check-in worked.
Never animate on re-queryBars and lists animate on first paint only — refiltering a table must not re-run the entrance.

Rules that never bend
No shrunken tables. Under 640px a table becomes cards. Horizontal scrolling a payroll table on a phone is not a design.
44px touch targets. On phones every action is at least 44px and sits in the lower two-thirds of the screen.
One primary action, pinned. Check in, Submit, Apply — always visible above the bottom nav, never scrolled away.
Filters move, they don't vanish. Toolbar on desktop, bottom sheet on phone, same options in the same order.
