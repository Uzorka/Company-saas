Phase 03 — decisions locked
Role is the navigationThe sidebar is generated from the role's permissions. Restricted items stay visible but locked, so people learn the system's shape instead of guessing at it.
One hero actionEvery dashboard has exactly one primary action in the header — check in, review exceptions, send payroll for approval, verify visits.
Session policy30-minute idle timeout with draft preservation. Expiry explains itself rather than dumping you at a bare login.
Multi-tenant readyThe org switcher sits above navigation from day one, even with a single workspace.
