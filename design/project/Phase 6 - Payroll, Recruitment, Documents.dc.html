How sensitivity is shown
◇ Company-wide Plain white card. Anyone signed in can open it — handbooks, policies, forms.
▲ Restricted Warm tint and an amber badge. Department-level — visible to HR, the HOD and the person it concerns.
✕ Confidential Red tint. Contracts, medical notes, disciplinary files. Every open is written to the audit log with your name.
Why tint, not just a badge
A badge is read only when someone is looking for it. A whole-card tint is read peripherally, so an HR officer sharing their screen in an open-plan office notices the red card before they click it. Colour never carries the meaning alone — every level has a glyph and a word too.
