What makes a workspace its own
Identity. Logo, name and one accent colour. The layout, type and spacing never change — a tenant customises their mark, not the product.
Data. Fully isolated. No cross-tenant query exists, including for platform admins, who see counts and status but not employee records.
Configuration. Departments, offices, geofences, leave types and payroll rates are all per-tenant.
Audit. Each tenant's log is theirs. Platform actions are logged separately, and visibly, in the tenant's own log.
